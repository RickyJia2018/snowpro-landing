SnowPro Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 November 2026

1. Responsible operator

Googuar Technology Inc. operates SnowPro and is responsible for its privacy practices. Email snowpro-support@googuar.com; mailing address: 5611 Goring St, Burnaby, BC V5B 0A3, Canada. This policy describes the app, website and related services; payment providers and external websites also have their own policies.

2. Information and purposes

We process account identifiers, email, verified phone information where provided, login-provider identifiers, credentials or security proofs, session/device information and notification tokens to create and protect accounts and deliver messages. Profile details, languages, selected country/region, resort preferences, instructor qualifications, portfolios and social links support discovery and requested services. Identity-document and selfie uploads support instructor application review; they are not public portfolio items.

We process lesson videos and feedback, course files, viewing progress, questions, chat text and attachments, reviews, carpool and buddy details, and reports to provide the features you use. Media processing can create thumbnails, transcoded files, transcripts and translated subtitles. Automated captions can be inaccurate. Purchase identifiers and receipts, credit balances, order and payout records, provider account identifiers, country and verification status support fulfilment, accounting, dispute handling and fraud prevention. Card or banking credentials entered directly with Apple, Google, Stripe or PayPal are handled by those providers; we receive the relevant transaction/account information, not a need for you to send us your full card number or password.

IP addresses, request times, app/device versions, errors, security events and policy-version/consent records help operate, troubleshoot and secure the service. We do not need your biometric template: operating-system biometric authentication returns a result to the app.

3. Location, permissions and advertising

Location data. With your device permission, SnowPro accesses precise or approximate device location (latitude and longitude from GPS, Wi-Fi or mobile networks, depending on your settings). When device location is unavailable, we may estimate your city or region from your IP address. We also process the places and coordinates you select or enter yourself.

Why we use it. When you find and view carpools, location helps select nearby ski resorts, find and sort nearby departures, calculate distances and display relevant trips. When you publish or edit a carpool, it helps fill in and choose departure, pickup, destination and drop-off locations on the map. Location also supports nearby ski-buddy discovery, address search and map navigation where used. Location requests support these features while you use them; SnowPro does not request background location permission to continuously track your trips.

Transmission and sharing. SnowPro sends coordinates used for nearby searches and selected trip locations to its servers to provide those features. The operating-system location service and map/address-search providers may receive your IP address, search terms and coordinates needed to resolve or display a place. Depending on the feature and region, these include Apple or Android location/geocoding services, OpenStreetMap-based services (including Photon/Komoot and OsmAnd/Nominatim), and IP-location providers ipapi.co or Baidu. IP-location requests expose your IP address to the provider; they do not require sending it your GPS coordinates. Published trip locations are shown according to the trip's visibility and participant-access rules. Your current device location is not automatically published as a live location feed; a location you choose to publish becomes part of that post. Only publish pickup or meeting details you are comfortable sharing.

Choice and retention. You can decline or revoke precise or approximate location permission in device settings and choose resorts or enter trip locations manually. Nearby sorting, distance accuracy and automatic location filling may be limited. Disabling GPS permission does not hide the IP address sent with network requests or prevent IP-based approximate location. Recent location results may be cached on the device or by the operating system. Published addresses and coordinates are stored with the relevant trip or post; request/security logs may contain related network information. These records follow the retention, deletion and legal-obligation rules in section 5. To request access or deletion, contact snowpro-support@googuar.com.

Camera, photo-library and microphone permissions allow the capture or selection of media you choose to submit. Notification permission controls device alerts, not whether an in-app record exists. Change permissions in device settings.

Where an advertising feature is offered, Google AdMob and its partners may process device/network identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and consent choices to serve, measure and protect ads. Applicable consent and tracking choices must be respected; accepting this policy alone is not consent to tracking or every optional use. Login, purchases and service notices must be distinguished from optional advertising choices.

4. Who can see information

Public profiles, instructor portfolios, published course previews, reviews and carpool/buddy posts can be seen by other users or visitors as the feature permits. Purchased course content is available to entitled users. Lesson submissions are made available to the participating student/guardian and instructor; chat content to its participants. Authorized support, moderation, security and dispute personnel, and service providers supporting these functions, may access relevant records when necessary. Private does not mean that only the instructor can ever access a file, and chat is not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Recipients can copy material outside our control; choose carefully what you share.

We use infrastructure and service providers such as Google Cloud and Cloudflare storage/delivery, Firebase authentication/messaging, JPush notification delivery, map/location services, email delivery and the payment providers used for your transaction. They receive information necessary for the enabled function, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements. Push previews may appear on a lock screen. External social links and map/payment sites have their own practices. Information may also be disclosed when lawfully required or necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights or safety, or implement an organizational transfer with legally required safeguards and notice. This policy is not blanket consent to unrelated disclosure.

5. International processing, security and retention

Providers and recipients may process information outside your province or country, where local authorities may lawfully access it. Applicable transfer safeguards, notices and consent requirements must be met; you may contact us for information about relevant providers and processing locations. We use access controls, session protections and other safeguards appropriate to the service, but no storage or transmission system is risk-free. Signed file links are access credentials and should not be shared publicly.

We keep information for the purpose for which it is needed, including providing purchased access, resolving disputes, maintaining security and meeting applicable accounting or legal obligations. Account deletion is staged: access is disabled and subsequent cleanup/anonymization runs after the account recovery period, subject to unresolved obligations and permitted retention. It is not a promise that every file, backup, payment-provider record or recipient copy disappears immediately. Temporary uploads and unreferenced media are cleaned up separately; a file still used by a valid service record may remain necessary. Backup copies, where retained, are restricted and expire under their retention lifecycle. We do not promise to retain every uploaded file indefinitely; keep your own originals.

6. Your choices, children and requests

Accounts are for adults: at least 18 and the age of majority where they live. A parent or legal guardian may submit a minor's video through the guardian's account with lawful authority; submit only what is necessary and avoid unnecessary identifiers. Contact us about an unauthorized child account or child-related content.

You can edit available profile fields, manage device permissions and notification preferences, use available blocking/reporting controls, and request account deletion. Contact snowpro-support@googuar.com for access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or other rights provided by your law, or to challenge our handling of information. We may verify identity proportionately and explain any lawful restriction or effect on a requested service. Consent withdrawal does not invalidate lawful earlier processing. You may complain to the relevant privacy regulator, including the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia where applicable. Material changes will be communicated as legally required; additional consent will be sought where required rather than inferred solely from this policy.

7. How to request account and associated-data deletion

Request deletion in Account → Edit Profile & Security → Delete Account, completing the account-email verification. You do not have to reinstall the app to make a request: visit https://snowpro.googuar.com/account-deletion.html or email snowpro-support@googuar.com with the subject "Delete my SnowPro account". Provide your account email or SnowPro ID and tell us if you want account deletion or deletion of specific data. We verify account control proportionately and explain any further information needed; never send passwords, login codes or full payment-card details. Restricted accounts and users who cannot sign in can use this support route.

The in-app process deactivates access and gives a 7-day recovery period before scheduled cleanup. Logging in and confirming restoration during that period cancels the deletion request. Profile identifiers and contact details, credentials/login links, device and push identifiers, and associated uploaded media, posts and messages are within the scope of a deletion request; some associated records require support review and cleanup rather than immediate automatic erasure. We will explain the handling of your request and any required retention.

We may retain records needed to resolve outstanding lessons, payments, refunds, payouts, disputes, fraud or security incidents, and records required for accounting or other legal obligations. These may include transaction/provider identifiers, amounts, order records, necessary consent/audit records and relevant evidence. Retention is limited to the applicable purpose and obligation; it does not authorize continued unrelated use of a deleted account's personal data. Backup expiry and recipient/payment-provider copies are described in section 5 and remain subject to applicable rights. Account deletion is not merely permanent account freezing, but it is also not a promise of instant erasure from every system.

Google Play purchase questions and refund requests can also be made through https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 or snowpro-support@googuar.com. Deletion does not itself refund purchases or cancel a provider subscription, if applicable. SnowCoins and the current fixed-term Carpool Pass are one-time purchases, not auto-renewing subscriptions.
