SnowPro Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

1. Responsible operator

Googuar Technology Inc. operates SnowPro and is responsible for its privacy practices. Email contact@snowpro.app; mailing address: 5611 Goring St, Burnaby, BC V5B 0A3, Canada. This policy describes the app, website and related services; payment providers and external websites also have their own policies.

2. Information and purposes

We process account identifiers, email, verified phone information where provided, login-provider identifiers, credentials or security proofs, session/device information and notification tokens to create and protect accounts and deliver messages. Profile details, languages, selected country/region, resort preferences, instructor qualifications, portfolios and social links support discovery and requested services. Identity-document and selfie uploads support instructor application review; they are not public portfolio items.

We process lesson videos and feedback, course files, viewing progress, questions, chat text and attachments, reviews, carpool and buddy details, and reports to provide the features you use. Media processing can create thumbnails, transcoded files, transcripts and translated subtitles. Automated captions can be inaccurate. Purchase identifiers and receipts, credit balances, order and payout records, provider account identifiers, country and verification status support fulfilment, accounting, dispute handling and fraud prevention. Card or banking credentials entered directly with Apple, Google, Stripe or PayPal are handled by those providers; we receive the relevant transaction/account information, not a need for you to send us your full card number or password.

IP addresses, request times, app/device versions, errors, security events and policy-version/consent records help operate, troubleshoot and secure the service. We do not need your biometric template: operating-system biometric authentication returns a result to the app.

3. Location, permissions and advertising

When you grant location access, location and selected places can support nearby resorts, maps and departure/destination selection. Places you publish in a trip or buddy post are visible to its audience; avoid publishing your home address unnecessarily. Declining a permission may limit its associated feature. Camera, photo-library and microphone permissions allow the capture or selection of media you choose to submit. Notification permission controls device alerts, not whether an in-app record exists. Change permissions in device settings.

Where an advertising feature is offered, Google AdMob and its partners may process device/network identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and consent choices to serve, measure and protect ads. Applicable consent and tracking choices must be respected; accepting this policy alone is not consent to tracking or every optional use. Login, purchases and service notices must be distinguished from optional advertising choices.

4. Who can see information

Public profiles, instructor portfolios, published course previews, reviews and carpool/buddy posts can be seen by other users or visitors as the feature permits. Purchased course content is available to entitled users. Lesson submissions are made available to the participating student/guardian and instructor; chat content to its participants. Authorized support, moderation, security and dispute personnel, and service providers supporting these functions, may access relevant records when necessary. Private does not mean that only the instructor can ever access a file, and chat is not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Recipients can copy material outside our control; choose carefully what you share.

We use infrastructure and service providers such as Google Cloud and Cloudflare storage/delivery, Firebase authentication/messaging, JPush notification delivery, map/location services, email delivery and the payment providers used for your transaction. They receive information necessary for the enabled function, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements. Push previews may appear on a lock screen. External social links and map/payment sites have their own practices. Information may also be disclosed when lawfully required or necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights or safety, or implement an organizational transfer with legally required safeguards and notice. This policy is not blanket consent to unrelated disclosure.

5. International processing, security and retention

Providers and recipients may process information outside your province or country, where local authorities may lawfully access it. Applicable transfer safeguards, notices and consent requirements must be met; you may contact us for information about relevant providers and processing locations. We use access controls, session protections and other safeguards appropriate to the service, but no storage or transmission system is risk-free. Signed file links are access credentials and should not be shared publicly.

We keep information for the purpose for which it is needed, including providing purchased access, resolving disputes, maintaining security and meeting applicable accounting or legal obligations. Account deletion is staged: access is disabled and subsequent cleanup/anonymization runs after the account recovery period, subject to unresolved obligations and permitted retention. It is not a promise that every file, backup, payment-provider record or recipient copy disappears immediately. Temporary uploads and unreferenced media are cleaned up separately; a file still used by a valid service record may remain necessary. Backup copies, where retained, are restricted and expire under their retention lifecycle. We do not promise to retain every uploaded file indefinitely; keep your own originals.

6. Your choices, children and requests

Accounts are for adults: at least 18 and the age of majority where they live. A parent or legal guardian may submit a minor's video through the guardian's account with lawful authority; submit only what is necessary and avoid unnecessary identifiers. Contact us about an unauthorized child account or child-related content.

You can edit available profile fields, manage device permissions and notification preferences, use available blocking/reporting controls, and request account deletion. Contact contact@snowpro.app for access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or other rights provided by your law, or to challenge our handling of information. We may verify identity proportionately and explain any lawful restriction or effect on a requested service. Consent withdrawal does not invalidate lawful earlier processing. You may complain to the relevant privacy regulator, including the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia where applicable. Material changes will be communicated as legally required; additional consent will be sought where required rather than inferred solely from this policy.
