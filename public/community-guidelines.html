8. Community and child safety standards

These rules apply to profiles, courses, lesson uploads, reviews, chat, carpool and ski-buddy posts, including private messages. Before uploading or sharing content, you must accept the applicable Terms of Service and follow these standards. Do not post sexually explicit content or sexual services, non-consensual intimate material, threats, bullying, discriminatory hate, graphic violence, scams, impersonation, doxxing, unlawful goods or services, or material that infringes another person's rights. Do not use payments, gifts or rewards to encourage prohibited content or conduct.

SnowPro prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, child sex trafficking, and imagery or other content that sexually exploits a child. This applies regardless of whether content is real, altered or computer-generated. Adult-only accounts and guardian-submitted sports footage do not create an exception. Never arrange unsupervised contact with a minor through SnowPro.

Use the in-app report controls on the relevant content, profile or conversation and the available block-user controls, or contact snowpro-support@googuar.com. For a child-safety report, use the subject "SnowPro child safety" and provide the content/user identifier, location within the app and relevant dates. Do not download, forward or attach suspected CSAM; provide identifiers so authorized reviewers can locate it. If a person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This inbox is not an emergency service.

We review reports, restrict or remove violating content, and may suspend or terminate accounts and take measures against repeat violations. We remove identified CSAM, preserve relevant evidence only as lawfully required, and report to the appropriate regional or national authority when required by applicable law. Reporters and affected users can contact support about a decision; we do not promise disclosure of another person's confidential information. Our child-safety contact route is snowpro-support@googuar.com, operated by Googuar Technology Inc. Published standards are available at https://snowpro.googuar.com/community-guidelines.html

8. 社区与儿童安全标准

本规则适用于个人资料、课程、教学上传、评价、聊天、拼车及雪搭子帖子，包括私信。上传或分享内容前，须接受适用的服务条款并遵守本规则。禁止色情或露骨性内容、性服务、未经同意的私密影像、威胁、欺凌、歧视性仇恨、血腥暴力、诈骗、冒充、公开他人私人信息、违法商品或服务，以及侵犯他人权利的内容。不得利用付款、礼物或奖励鼓励违规内容或行为。

SnowPro 禁止儿童性虐待与性剥削（CSAE）及儿童性虐待材料（CSAM），包括为性剥削而诱导儿童、向未成年人进行性招揽、性勒索、儿童性贩运，以及对儿童进行性剥削的影像或其他内容。无论内容真实、经修改或由计算机生成，均适用。成年人账号或监护人提交的体育视频不构成例外。不得通过 SnowPro 安排与未成年人无监护接触。

可在相关内容、资料或会话内使用举报及屏蔽功能，或联系 snowpro-support@googuar.com。儿童安全举报请注明邮件主题“SnowPro 儿童安全”，提供内容／用户标识、App 内位置及相关日期。不要下载、转发或附上疑似 CSAM；请提供标识供有权限的审核人员定位。有人面临即时危险时，请联系当地紧急服务；此邮箱不是紧急救助渠道。

我们审核举报、限制或移除违规内容，并可暂停或终止账号，对重复违规采取措施。发现 CSAM 后会予以移除，仅依法保存必要证据，并按适用法律要求向有关地区或国家主管机构报告。举报人及受影响用户可联系支持渠道复核决定；我们不承诺披露他人的保密信息。儿童安全联系渠道为 Googuar Technology Inc. 运营的 snowpro-support@googuar.com。公开规则：https://snowpro.googuar.com/community-guidelines.html。
