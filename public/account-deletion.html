Delete your SnowPro account and data

Updated: 10 November 2026 · 中文

Email a deletion request / 发送删除申请

If no email app opens, copy snowpro-support@googuar.com into any email service and send the same request. This page does not require a SnowPro login or app installation. Clicking the email button prepares a message; you must send it to submit your request.

Request deletion without the app

SnowPro is operated by Googuar Technology Inc. You can request deletion of your SnowPro account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app or signing in to this website.

Email snowpro-support@googuar.com with the subject “Delete my SnowPro account”. Include your account email or SnowPro ID, and say whether you want to delete the account and its associated data or specific personal data. If your account uses an Apple relay email, provide the saved relay email or SnowPro ID if known. If you cannot access that mailbox or your account is restricted, explain this so support can arrange proportionate ownership verification. Never email your password, login code, ID-document photo or full payment-card details.

Delete from the app

Open Account → Edit Profile & Security → Delete Account. Read the notice and complete the account-email verification. An email being sent does not delete the account: confirm the deletion action. Uninstalling the app or logging out does not delete it.

Timing and recovery

A verified in-app deletion request deactivates access and starts a 7-day recovery period. You can cancel that request by signing in and confirming restoration during the period. Scheduled cleanup follows; some associated records need support review. For an email request, support verifies account control and explains when deletion is scheduled, any recovery period and further action required. We will explain any outstanding obligation or necessary retention rather than treating temporary deactivation as completed deletion.

Data in scope and retention

Your request covers account/profile and contact information, credentials and linked-login data, device and push identifiers, and associated personal data in uploaded videos/photos, posts and messages. Automated cleanup does not instantly erase every related record; support can handle associated-data requests. We may retain necessary payment/order/refund/payout records, consent/audit and security records, dispute evidence and other records required for accounting, fraud prevention, safety or legal obligations, limited to those purposes. Where a service provider processes personal data on our behalf, we request deletion of that data as part of handling your deletion request, subject to applicable lawful retention. Restricted backup copies expire under their retention lifecycle. Independent recipients and payment providers may have their own legal retention obligations; these do not justify retaining unrelated personal data on our systems. Contact support for the data and retention applicable to your request. The Privacy Policy explains these practices.

Purchases and help

Account deletion does not automatically refund a purchase or cancel any provider subscription. Current SnowCoin packs and fixed-term Carpool Passes are one-time purchases without auto-renewal. For a Google Play refund, see https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 or contact support. To ask about deletion, request a status update or raise a concern, email snowpro-support@googuar.com. This channel is not an emergency service.

Email a deletion request / 发送删除申请

无法唤起邮件应用时，可复制 snowpro-support@googuar.com，通过任意邮箱发送申请。此页面无需登录 SnowPro，也无需安装 App。点击按钮仅准备邮件，实际发送后才提交申请。

不使用 App 也能申请删除

SnowPro 由 Googuar Technology Inc. 运营。无需重新安装 App 或登录此网站，即可申请删除 SnowPro 账号及关联个人数据。

请向 snowpro-support@googuar.com 发送主题为“删除我的 SnowPro 账号”的邮件，提供账号邮箱或 SnowPro ID，并说明删除整个账号及关联数据还是特定个人数据。使用 Apple 隐藏邮箱的账号，可提供保存的中继邮箱或 SnowPro ID（如已知）。无法访问该邮箱或账号受限时请说明，支持人员会安排适度的所有权核验。不要发送密码、登录验证码、证件照片或完整银行卡信息。

App 内删除路径

进入“账号 → 编辑个人资料与安全 → 删除账号”，阅读提示并完成账号邮箱验证。发送验证码邮件不等于删除，须确认删除操作。卸载或退出登录不等于注销。

处理时间与恢复

通过验证的 App 内注销申请会停用访问，并开始 7 天恢复期；在此期间登录并确认恢复，可取消申请。随后执行计划清理，部分关联记录需要客服审核。通过邮件申请时，支持人员核验账号控制权后会说明预计处理时间、适用恢复期及后续步骤。存在未决义务或必要保留时会说明，不会将临时停用视为已完成删除。

数据范围与保留

删除申请涵盖账号／资料、联系方式、凭证及登录关联、设备与推送标识，以及上传视频／照片、帖子和消息中的关联个人数据。自动清理不会立即抹除所有关联记录，客服可处理关联数据删除请求。为记账、防欺诈、安全、争议处理或法律义务，可在必要范围保留支付／订单／退款／提现、同意及审计、安全与证据等记录，并限于这些目的。对于代我们处理个人数据的服务商，处理删除申请时也会要求其删除相关数据，依法必须保留的情况除外。受限备份按保留周期到期。独立接收者及支付平台可能有其自身的法律保留义务，但这不构成我们继续保留无关个人资料的理由。具体数据与期限可联系支持渠道，详细说明见隐私政策。

购买与帮助

注销不会自动退款或取消支付平台订阅。目前的雪币包和固定期限 Carpool Pass 均为不自动续订的单次购买。Google Play 退款可查阅 https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 或联系支持人员。申请进度、删除疑问或投诉可发至 snowpro-support@googuar.com；此渠道不是紧急救助服务。
